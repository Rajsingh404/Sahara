# Name-call evaluation

38 "Aryan" clips, 69 similar-name clips and 105 three-second background pieces (TV, kitchen, hawkers, temple, rain, door and drawer) from recorders advika, mahi, prachi, raj (50 similar-name clips from the second upload have no named recorder). Generated by `scripts/eval_name_call.py`.

The 50 clips without a named recorder are spread evenly over the folds below: each is scored once and never trained on in the fold that scores it.

## 1. Spotting "Aryan" from a voice the model never heard

Leave one recorder out: the head is trained on the other three recorders' clips and scenes, then scored on the fourth.

| Negatives | ROC AUC | Equal error rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Background scenes (TV, kitchen, street...) | 0.872 | 19% |
| Similar names (Arya, Aryanshi, other names) | 0.743 | 32% |
| Both | 0.821 | 24% |
| Similar names, named recorders only (19 clips) | 0.755 | 37% |
| Similar names, second upload only (50 clips) | 0.738 | 32% |

At the equal-error threshold (0.62): 79% of calls caught, 15% of background pieces and 36% of similar names raise a false call.

False calls per background scene at that threshold:

| Scene | Pieces | False calls |
|---|---:|---:|

| hawker | 23 | 8 |
| kitchen | 17 | 0 |

| temple | 15 | 0 |

## 3. A caller who is not enrolled

Leave one caller out entirely. Below the accept threshold the app says "Someone is calling you". Naming the wrong person is worse than "Someone" (the user still learns someone is calling), so the app defaults to 0.70.

| Accept threshold | Enrolled callers named correctly | Unenrolled callers shown as "Someone" |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.60 | 28/38 | 16% |
| 0.65 | 28/38 | 39% |
| 0.70 | 19/38 | 82% |
| 0.75 | 10/38 | 97% |
| 0.80 | 2/38 | 100% |

## Caveats

- Each recorder used their own phone and room, so caller identity here is partly phone and room identity. Re-run after the family enrols on Aryan's own phone, which is how the app is used.
- "Recorder" stands in for "caller": the clips do not say who spoke. If a recorder filmed someone else, that clip is counted under the recorder.
- The spotter is trained for one name. Another user's name needs its own clips (or synthetic TTS clips) and a retrain.
